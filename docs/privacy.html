Privacy Policy

WordQuest · Last updated 15 October 2026 · Support

WordQuest is a word search and trivia game made by Anthony Mamati. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: there are no accounts, we don't run any servers, and we never see your personal information.

Information we don't collect

WordQuest does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos, and it does not send your gameplay data to us.

Game progress

Your levels, stars and settings are stored on your device. If iCloud is on, they also sync between your devices through Apple's iCloud key-value storage in your own iCloud account. We can't access it.

Game Center

Leaderboards and achievements use Apple Game Center, which is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Your Game Center nickname and scores are visible to other players according to your Game Center settings.

Local multiplayer

Local multiplayer connects directly to nearby devices on your local network. The player name you choose and in-game answers are shared only with the players in your room and are not sent to us or stored anywhere else.

Advertising

WordQuest is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Every ad request is marked as child-directed and limited to general-audience content, and the app never asks to track you across other companies' apps (it doesn't use App Tracking Transparency or the advertising identifier). To serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical information such as your IP address, device type, operating system and how you interact with ads. In regions where consent is required (for example the EU, UK and some US states), Google's consent form asks for your choice first, and you can change it later from Settings › Privacy Options in the app. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Children

WordQuest is suitable for all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children, and ads are served in Google's child-directed mode.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
