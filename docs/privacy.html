Privacy Policy

WordQuest · Last updated 31 October 2026 · Support

WordQuest is a word search and trivia game made by Anthony Mamati. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: there are no accounts, we don't run any servers, and we never see your personal information.

Information we don't collect

WordQuest does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos, and it does not send your gameplay data to us.

Game progress

Your levels, stars and settings are stored on your device. If iCloud is on, they also sync between your devices through Apple's iCloud key-value storage in your own iCloud account. We can't access it.

Game Center

Leaderboards and achievements use Apple Game Center, which is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Your Game Center nickname and scores are visible to other players according to your Game Center settings.

Local multiplayer

Local multiplayer connects directly to nearby devices on your local network. The player name you choose and in-game answers are shared only with the players in your room and are not sent to us or stored anywhere else.

Advertising

WordQuest is free and shows ads from Meta Audience Network on both iPhone/iPad and Android. To choose, deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Meta may collect your IP address (and the approximate location it implies), device and app information, and how you interact with ads.

On iPhone and iPad, the app never asks to track you across other companies' apps (it doesn't request App Tracking Transparency permission), so Meta can't use your device's advertising identifier and the ads you see aren't based on your activity in other apps.

On Android, Meta may also collect your device's advertising identifier and use it to show ads based on your interests. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android device settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads).

See Meta's Privacy Policy and how Meta ads work.

Children

WordQuest is a general-audience game and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. On Android, the app is listed for players aged 13 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
