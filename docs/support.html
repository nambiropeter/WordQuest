WordQuest · Privacy Policy

Questions, bug reports or ideas? Email anthonynambiropeter@gmail.com, ideally with your device model and iOS version. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

My progress didn't carry over to my new device. Progress syncs through iCloud. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, then open WordQuest on both devices with an internet connection.

Local multiplayer can't find my friend. Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi network and WordQuest needs Local Network access (Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network).

Leaderboards aren't showing. Sign in to Game Center in the Settings app, then reopen WordQuest.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? Where the law requires it, a Privacy Options row appears in WordQuest's Settings screen.
